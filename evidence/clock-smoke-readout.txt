# The design sends one 49-byte line every 2^25 sys_clk cycles (671.08864 ms at
# 50 MHz).  c27 and hk are COUNTS of bit-12 edges of cnt_27 and cnt_hclk: bit 12

# of cycles and the reader never has to know which bit was read.  The slow
# cadence is forced by the link: the one-wire path carries about 100-170 B/s.
